## Faults these runs show, ranked by what a user would notice

1. **The user asked for a helper and for safety on a password-and-money job,
   and got neither.** B's lead edited `server.js` itself, ran its own tests
   and committed in 56 seconds, with no review and without saying it had
   skipped one. The first-prompt card says "Buy independent review for money,
   auth ..." and also "Do a step yourself when it fits in about eight tool
   calls", and the lead followed the second. No hook noticed: the `Stop` hooks
   were silent, and the review hold only exists for a dispatched helper.
   Likely fix: `skills/orchestrate/scripts/lib/card.mjs` (say the eight-call
   rule never covers money or sign-in, and that an explicit "get a helper"
   wins), and a finish check in `skills/orchestrate/scripts/turn-check.mjs`
   for commits that touch auth or money with no reviewer this session.
2. **A free-form risky return can never be held for review.** The hold keys
   on the dispatch's tool-use id, and the returned row always has
   `toolUseId: null` in a live session (A's records, three of three). The
   match is available by `agentId`, which both rows carry. Fix in
   `skills/orchestrate/scripts/ledger.mjs` (fill `toolUseId` from the
   dispatch row with the same `agentId`), or match by `agentId` in
   `turn-check.mjs` `freeFormOpen`.
3. **The three plain lines never reach a lead that does not open the skill.**
   A went straight to helpers with one sentence; B said nothing before
   building. The rule lives only in `skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md`; the likely
   fix is a line in the first-prompt card (`skills/orchestrate/scripts/lib/card.mjs`).
4. **Helper worktrees and branches are left behind after the merge, and the
   user is not told.** Repo A ends with three `worktree-agent-…` branches and
   three folders under `.claude/worktrees`, plus an untracked `.claude/` the
   lead chose to leave out of the commit. Likely fix: the merge step in
   `skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md` and the card, or a reminder from
   `turn-check.mjs` when merged helper branches remain.
5. **Three helpers took nine dispatch calls.** A's lead first sent
   `general-purpose` three times (refused: "general-purpose has no turn cap and
   can start helpers of its own. Send a capped role agent instead ..."), then
   `orch-implementer` without a worktree three times (refused: "orch-implementer
   always works in its own worktree and branch ... Say WHERE: … worktree: yes"),
   then the three that ran. Nothing in the first card says which helper type or
   that it needs a worktree. Likely fix: `lib/card.mjs`, or let
   `guard-agent.mjs` add the worktree itself for `orch-implementer`.
6. **The lead gets the whole 2 KB return, not the short form.** Filing works,
   but the host hands the full hand-back to the lead, and `ledger.mjs` is
   silent on purpose. Three returns cost the lead about 6.6 KB instead of
   three status lines. Likely fix: the helper agents' return instructions
   (`skills/orchestrate/assets/agents/orch-*.md`: put only the five lines in the hand-back, the rest in
   the file) rather than the ledger.
7. **The lead's checkpoint lands where the plugin does not look.** C's lead
   expanded `~/.claude/orchestrate/...` under the project's memory folder, so
   the plugin kept saying "newest checkpoint: none" and wrote its own note
   from the transcript instead. Likely fix: give an absolute path in the ask
   (`skills/orchestrate/scripts/lib/context-advice.mjs`, `shortPath`).
8. **The capacity figure ignores the window.** With a 100,000 window the cards
   said "~180k" all session and compaction fired at ~153k; the promised
   threshold also jumped from "next: compact ~135k" to "next: autocompact
   ~180k" (`lib/context-advice.mjs`).
9. **Plugin words in user-visible text.** "worktree scaffolding" (A),
   "per the context reminder" (C). Minor; card wording in `lib/card.mjs`.
