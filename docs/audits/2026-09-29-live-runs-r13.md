## Faults, ranked by what a user would notice

1. **The plain three-line plan before helpers is still skipped** (item 1) even
   though the card now says it. Both leads wrote one sentence. Likely fix:
   `skills/orchestrate/scripts/lib/card.mjs` (move the sentence next to "hand over the rest",
   or make the dispatch guard note in `guard-agent.mjs` say it once, on the first helper dispatch).
2. **Helpers hand back 1.3 to 1.9 KB in the TASK / STATUS form, not the five
   lines** (item 3). Two contradictory return rules sit in the same helper
   file. Likely fix: `skills/orchestrate/assets/agents/orch-implementer.md`
   lines 67-70 (drop or subordinate "Return in the packet's schema …", keep
   the five lines), `orch-browser.md` has the same pair; the other role files
   have only the five-line rule (the reviewer, which has no second rule, did
   hand back in five lines).
3. **Plugin words still reach the user** (item 11): "worktree" in five lead
   messages across A and B, "harness" once. Likely fix: `lib/card.mjs`
   (say "helper's separate folder" in the user-facing words rule) and the
   finish reminder text.
4. **Leftover helper branches are not reported and the lead's closing claim
   overstates the tidy-up** (items 5 and 10). Likely fix: the merge-and-clean
   step in `skills/orchestrate/SKILL.md`, and a reminder in
   `skills/orchestrate/scripts/turn-check.mjs` naming leftover `worktree-agent-…`
   branches, not only folders. Also decide whether a forced folder removal
   should be refused: at this commit it was allowed.
5. **The capacity figure is single but wrong for a set window and disappears
   past it** (item 7): "next: compact ~135k" against a real compaction at
   152,896 with a 100,000 window; the card at ~140k drops the figure.
   Likely fix: `skills/orchestrate/scripts/lib/context-advice.mjs`.
6. **The review hold fires when the review is already running, and marks the
   reviewer's own dispatch as needing review** (item 4). Likely fix:
   `skills/orchestrate/scripts/turn-check.mjs` (count a dispatched
   orch-reviewer whose REVIEW OF names the helper's agent id as an answer, or
   wait until the review returns before speaking) and `guard-agent.mjs` (do
   not add the "will wait for an independent review" line to an
   `orch-reviewer` dispatch). Also `ledger.mjs`: the reviewer's return is
   recorded as `status: null`, `evidence: false`.
