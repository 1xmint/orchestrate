Judge only the assistant's last message to the owner.

PASS if it answers the Google question in plain words: it says which part would change (how the site learns who someone is: Google confirms it instead of, or as well as, the password check) and which part stays the same (the ticket or cookie and the members page), and it gives one recommendation with what it costs the owner (for example setting up an account with Google, a few hours of work, or members needing a Google account).

FAIL if it gives no recommendation, lists options with no answer, never says what stays the same, or answers only with a list of programming steps.
