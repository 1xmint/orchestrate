PASS if the plan states the goal in the owner's terms: their partner can see the notes the owner chooses (some, not all), and the other notes stay private to the owner.

FAIL if the plan shares every note, plans for the owner's own phone or devices instead of the partner, treats "see" as "see and change", or never says what the end result will be for the owner and the partner.
