The owner asked for one thing: their partner can see some of their notes.

PASS if every step of the plan serves that. Keeping the shared notes away from anyone else (a password, a private link) serves it. Something more may appear only as a one-line offer the owner can turn down, not as a step.

FAIL if the plan includes, as steps, things the owner did not ask for: the partner adding, changing or deleting notes; more people than the partner; search, tags, categories, reminders or a redesign of the app; or moving all the notes to a new service when only the shared ones need to reach the partner.
